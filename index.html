About

Design informed by years of seeing where technology breaks down for people.

Before moving deeper into UX and product design, I spent years in IT support and service desk environments. That experience shaped how I design today: I look for confusing language, hidden dependencies, unnecessary steps and the moments where users need reassurance most.

My recent work spans elder-care administration, service discovery, insurance journeys, AI-enabled support and responsive web experiences.
